# OFL course material: time-and-sales (text extracted from PDF)

-------------------------------------------------- 
**TIME & SALES** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
Time and Sales is a record that documents every transaction or trade executed (filled). It provides the following 
details: 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**INFORMATION DISPLAYED** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
Time 
- The precise moment the trade was executed. 

Price 
- The price at which the transaction occurred. 

Size 
- The volume of the trade, typically measured in lots. 

Direction 
- An indication of whether the trade was a buy or a sell, often shown through color coding or highlighting to 
signify whether it's filling or sweeping the order book. 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**REAL-TIME MARKET INFORMATION** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
This offers an immediate reflection of market activity compared to the Depth of Market (DOM), illustrating what 
transactions are actually occurring and the extent of price movement. 

Together with the dynamics of orders being added or removed from the DOM, this information sheds light on 
potential market absorption or initiative activities, based on actual order fills and observed price progression.
