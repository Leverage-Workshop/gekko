**DOMINATOR 2.0** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**Definition:**   
This study reflects the original Dominator function but incorporates session-aware activity. 

It evaluates behavior within the same period from prior days (for example, Initial Balance vs. prior Initial 
Balance activity). This provides insight into whether aggression in the current session is truly anomalous 
relative to historical context, rather than relative to unrelated periods. 

Even within RTH hours, there are distinct changes in pace and volume between morning, midday, and 
end-of-day activity. By assessing each period against its own unique distribution, Dominator 2.0 enhances 
precision and provides signals calibrated to the true behavior of that session type. 

Usage: 

Dominator 2.0 paints an arrow on your chart when it detects disproportionate strength on the buy side (up 
arrow) or sell side (down arrow). 

The pace, size, and intensity required to signify disproportionate strength are calculated using our proprietary 
algorithm, which now compares analogous session behavior rather than 24-hour response. 

Like all execution tools, Dominator 2.0 should be leveraged alongside key structural references to maximize 
efficacy. 

**Summary:**   
It is recommended to place emphasis on structural references when prints occur, as this minimizes risk during 
the identification of market participation. 

Dominator 2.0 plays a critical role by highlighting when aggression aligns with structural levels. By combining 
structural confluence with anomaly detection, the probability of continuation or reversal is heightened, leading 
to more confident and disciplined decision-making. 

**Nuance:**   
Within the recommended settings for Dominator 2.0, prints will be more abundant and will display highlighted 
outlier responses relative to the timeframe traded.
