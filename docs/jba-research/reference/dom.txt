**DEPTH OF MARKET (DOM)** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
The Depth of Market (DOM) serves as a transparent visualization into the order activity necessary to define a 
setup. As a centralized order book, it organizes all bid (buy) and offer (sell) orders in accordance with a 
"price-time priority" rule. 

This fundamental trading principle pledges market fairness by prioritizing the earliest-placed orders for 
execution and maintaining full visibility of all orders. 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**DOM STRUCTURE** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
In the DOM layout, the bid orders are displayed in the column on the left, while the ask orders are seen in the 
column on the right. 

Monitoring these columns will provide insight into market participation and anticipate potential price trends. 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**PULLING & STACKING** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
An exclusive attribute of the DOM is the Pulling and Stacking mechanism. This represents live modifications 
made by limit orders for buying or selling. 

These alterations provide a snapshot of the market's immediate preference. Spotting significant resting limit 
orders being added or withdrawn equates to checking the market's pulse, which helps gauge trading interest at 
selected price levels. 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**SPREAD & LIQUIDITY** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
The DOM's Spread is the distance between the bid and the ask. 

It could be as minuscule as a single tick, but in times of high volatility or major news events, you may witness 
multiple ticks between the optimal bid and offer. The spread serves as a measure of market liquidity. 

-------------------------------------------------- 
**ORDER TYPES** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
The DOM accommodates a variety of order types including: 

- Limit orders   
- Market orders   
- Good Til Date   
- Fill or Kill   
- Market on Close   
- Limit on Close   
- Stop order   
- Iceberg order   
- Block Order   

 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Each of these order types suits distinct trading strategies, giving traders more control over their market 
engagements. 
-------------------------------------------------- 
**SWEEPING** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
Sweeping is a circumstance where a sizable market order depletes all available orders at the recent market 
price and starts filling at subsequent prices. 

This demonstrates a potential for initiative activity to buy or sell based upon the progress and response of 
price. 

-------------------------------------------------- 
**DOM CONCEPTS** 
-------------------------------------------------- 
Large Orders   
- Large orders like iceberg or block orders can leave an imprint on the market.   
- These orders can sway price direction but should not sway trading preference until responsive activity occurs 
as a result from the particular zone. 

Layering   
- To visualize layering, it is necessary to see numerous price levels having large quantities of orders to attempt 
to mitigate considerable market impact.   
- It also conceals the actual size of the order. 

Reloading   
- This occurs when orders persistently replace their bid or offer as their limit orders get filled.   
- This could denote absorption, showing buying or selling interest at that particular price level. 

Absorption   
- This signifies a scenario where large limit orders (or refreshing limit orders) soak up opposing market orders, 
restraining further price movement.   
- This can display a potential reversal in the price activity. 

Initiative Activity   
- This is a display of orders replenishing and also stepping up/down the order book to pursue their desired 
price.   
- Evident as limit orders begin climbing or descending the price ladder in size as the book thins out.   
- This can indicate a powerful buying or selling momentum.
