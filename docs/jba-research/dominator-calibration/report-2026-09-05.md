# Dominator 2.0 settings review, 2026-09-05

Five Dominator 2.0 settings on the NQ execution chart, scored over 44 trading sessions
(2026-07-07 to 2026-09-04) from the hidden volume-bar charts' own exports. Method, data and the
full tables are in [`README.md`](./README.md); `score-dnd.mjs` regenerates every number here. The
operator trades 08:30 to 14:00 CST, so that window is the basis for every verdict; the overnight
gets one section at the end.

**How to read the numbers.** For each print event, the reference is the close of the print's bar,
taken at that bar's end. "Push left" is how much further price went in the print's direction
within the next 60 minutes, "peaks after" when that extreme came, "comes back" how far it then
retraced from the extreme, and "round trip" how often it came all the way back to the print. The
continuation ratio is the median push divided by the median move against, where every ordinary bar
in the session scores 1.0 and 50 percent.

## The one finding that organises everything else

The window length is the setting that matters, not the volume.

| Window | What a print means | Push left | Peaks after | Comes back | Round trip |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 60 minutes (three settings) | a push with room left | 75 to 78 pts | 33 to 42 min | 71 to 92 pts | 48 to 54% |
| 30 minutes (two settings) | a push about to turn | 38 to 46 pts | 22 to 28 min | 80 to 87 pts | 63 to 67% |

This holds in July and in August separately for all five settings. A 60-minute print is a
"do not fight it for most of an hour" flag. A 30-minute print is the "print, no progress, turn"
setup from the 05-04 replay, and when a 30-minute print re-prints on consecutive bars and then
stops, the turn is close: the extreme is in within 25 to 33 points and the next one to three bars
(17 to 18 minutes timed at bar end), then a move about twice that the other way (n 21 to 23 per
setting, so indicative).

The second finding is that **a print nobody else agrees with is noise**. Every setting's solo
prints, with no other setting printing the same side within 10 minutes, score at or below the
ordinary bar. With one or more agreeing, the 60-minute settings score 1.5 to 3.2 with 58 to 68
percent continuation. Agreement is measured on the moment each print is certainly known, the end
of its first bar, and a confirmed event is scored from the bar in which the last confirming print
became known, so no price movement before the confirmation is credited to it. One nuance: a
30-minute print that two or more other settings confirm also continues (1.5 to 1.6, about 60
percent); it is the lone or singly-confirmed 30-minute print that turns.

## Setting by setting

### White, 7250 volume, 60 minutes

| Events | Per session | Longest gap | Buy / sell | Ratio 60 min | Continues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 65 | 1.5 | 4 sessions | 24 / 41 | 1.54 | 63% |

The heaviest setting and the rarest. Its edge is concentrated in two places: the opening half
hour, where its prints extend a median 146 points and continue 70 percent of the time (n 20), and
prints that another setting agrees with (ratio 1.8 to 3.2, 59 to 68 percent, n 51). Alone, after
09:00, it is near the ordinary bar (1.10). Its strings are the longest, about 30 minutes, and the
push still has about 65 points and 40 minutes in it when the string ends, so there is little cost
to waiting for the string.
Overnight it is below baseline in both Asia and Europe.

**Change warranted: no dial change.** Keep it, and read it as the opening print and the
confirming print. Since the 7250 chart already exists, a third instance with a 2-hour window can
be added to it at no bookkeeping cost; see the test below.

### Magenta, 7250 volume, 30 minutes

| Events | Per session | Longest gap | Buy / sell | Ratio 60 min | Continues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 81 | 1.8 | 2 sessions | 44 / 37 | 0.81 | 48% |

The balanced one, and the turn marker. After a print the push has a median 46 points and about
28 minutes left, then comes back 80, all the way 63 percent of the time. From the end of a
multi-bar string: 25 points and one to three bars, then back 64, round trip 78 percent (n 23).
One other setting agreeing does *not* make it a continuation print (0.70); only two or more do
(1.52, n 25), which is
consistent with it marking exhaustion rather than initiative. Its eleven opening prints were
followed by a median 147 points against, small n but the same direction.

**Change warranted: no dial change, a reading change.** Keep it, on the same chart as white, and
stop reading it as a push. It says the turn is about half an hour and 45 points away, or, when a
string ends, that it is a few bars away.

### Cyan, 6750 volume, 30 minutes

| Events | Per session | Longest gap | Buy / sell | Ratio 60 min | Continues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 91 | 2.1 | 3 sessions | 43 / 48 | 0.58 | 37% |

The same instrument as magenta with slightly more prints: it shares 49 of its 91 events with
magenta, its turn profile is marginally stronger (push left 38, back 87, round trip 67 percent) and
its continuation numbers are the weakest of the five on every measure. It is the only setting on
its own dedicated chart that adds nothing the others do not.

**Change warranted: yes.** Retire the 6750 / 30-minute study. Rather than delete the chart,
repurpose it: set it to 6250 volume and run the 2-hour window on it. That is the one setting Job
fully specified for NQ, and the data says longer windows read pushes better, so 2 hours is the
direction to extend. It also keeps the chart count at three.

### Yellow, 5250 volume, 60 minutes

| Events | Per session | Longest gap | Buy / sell | Ratio 60 min | Continues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 112 | 2.5 | 2 sessions | 39 / 73 | 1.58 | 61% |

The workhorse. Steadiest through the day (1.49 outside the open, 2.02 in it), both sides carry
the pattern (buy 1.68 and 69 percent, sell 1.51 and 56 percent), and it improved from July (1.55)
to August (1.76). Push left 76 points over 36 minutes, comes back 76, round trip half the time.
Strings are rare (103 of 112 events are one bar) and short. With one other setting agreeing it
scores 3.19 and 67 percent; alone, 1.01. Overnight in Asia it is the only setting above baseline
(1.50, coarse).

**Change warranted: no.** Leave it exactly as it is. If only one Dominator survived, this is the
one.

### Red, 4500 volume, 60 minutes

| Events | Per session | Longest gap | Buy / sell | Ratio 60 min | Continues |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 114 | 2.6 | 1 session | 43 / 71 | 1.50 | 56% |

The lightest and most frequent, and yellow's echo: 52 of its 114 events have a same-side yellow
print within 10 minutes. Its profile is yellow's (push 75, peak 33 minutes, back 92, round trip 54
percent) with a weaker open (1.13 against yellow's 2.02) and a larger solo share: 41 of 114 events
are solo, and those score 0.73. When something agrees it is good (1.56 to 2.50, 58 to 67 percent).

**Change warranted: yes, one of two ways.** Either retire it and save the chart, since yellow
already covers its useful prints, or keep it under one rule: a lone red print is ignored. Do not
lower its volume to get more prints; the extra prints would be the solo kind. Do not raise it
toward 5250; it would echo yellow harder.

## The recommended configuration

Three DND charts instead of four, four live studies instead of five, plus one test:

| Chart | Studies | Reading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7250 volume | 60 min (white), 30 min (magenta) | white: push with room, best at the open and when confirmed; magenta: turn coming, or turn here when a string ends |
| 5250 volume | 60 min (yellow) | the workhorse push print |
| 6250 volume (was 6750) | 2 hour, new colour | **test**: Job's published NQ setting, score it after two weeks of prints |
| 4500 volume | 60 min (red) | optional; if kept, lone red prints are ignored |

Rules that come out of the data for 08:30 to 14:00:

1. A single print from any setting is not a signal. Two settings, same side, within 10 minutes,
   is the signal, and it counts from the moment the second one is known.
2. After a confirmed 60-minute print, do not fade the push for half an hour or more; it has about
   75 points left and comes all the way back only half the time.
3. A 30-minute print says the turn is about half an hour and 40 to 45 points away. A 30-minute
   string that stops says the turn is within the next few bars.
4. Opening prints (08:30 to 09:00) from white or yellow are the strongest pushes in the data;
   opening prints from the 30-minute settings are the strongest turns.
5. The print price is the bar extreme plus or minus exactly 5 points. Take the 5 off before using
   it as a level.

## Overnight, for completeness

The exports are full Globex sessions, but overnight the volume bars run about an hour each, so a
60-minute window covers one bar and the resolution is too coarse to trust. On what there is: the
30-minute settings print more overnight than in the day (magenta 112 events in Asia against 81 in
the day) and score at the ordinary bar; white is below baseline in both Asia and Europe (0.63 and
0.73); yellow is the only setting above baseline in Asia (1.50, n 41) and it is below in Europe. Nothing here changes a day-session verdict, and nothing
here is strong enough to trade on.

## Confidence and what is still missing

- Per-setting event counts are 65 to 114 in the day session, and the window-length split holds in
  both halves of the sample. The agreement, opening and end-of-string splits are n 10 to 45 and
  could move at the next re-score.
- Windows count only volume bars that complete inside them, so a 60-minute figure is really
  "the last completed bar before 60 minutes", a few minutes short in the day session. Medians of
  even-sized samples average the two middle values. Peak times and agreement are timed at the end
  of the bar concerned, the conservative observable, which puts every "peaks after" figure one bar
  later than the extreme itself. These were Codex gate findings on the first two commits; they
  moved figures by a few points and one bar and changed no verdict.
- Nothing is scored against structure yet. Job's actual scorecard is prints at a level you had
  already drawn. The next step is joining events to the session's MGI levels from the bundles, so
  the numbers above become "at structure" numbers.
- Re-export the five DND charts and re-run `score-dnd.mjs` in two weeks before changing anything
  else, and score the 2-hour test on the same footing.
