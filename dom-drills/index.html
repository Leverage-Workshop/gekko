DOM Drills

Each drill drops you at a planned level and plays out one of Job's sequences. The opponent refreshes, pulls, and then your side either takes the level or it doesn't. Get in on the take and sit out the fakes. You get a grade after every drill.

The read, in five steps
  1. Arrive. Only read the DOM once price is in the planned band. Structure precedes execution.
  2. Defend. The offer at one price keeps reloading as buyers lift it (green stack flashes there), and sells make no new low. The opponent is active. Wait.
  3. Withdraw. Size at that price leaves without trades, so the pull/stack column flashes red. On its own this is not yet an entry.
  4. Take. The bid steps above that price, trades there, and holds. This is the entry.
  5. Accept. It should go your way right away. A trade back below the old offer price means scratch.

Shorts are the mirror: watch the bid, enter when the offer steps below it. The ladder shows size at each price, recent volume sold into the bid and bought from the offer, and pull/stack (P/S): how much resting size was added or removed at each price over the last few seconds, not counting trades. Red means pulled and green means stacked. A bright red flash is a sudden pull. Coach mode names each phase live while you learn.
